A little corner of Seoul in Kapsoya

Jowa is a Korean bakery and food court. Every morning the oven turns out soft donuts, cookies and pound cakes. Through the day the kitchen makes Korean favourites like kimbap and tteokbokki.

Kimchi and kimbap are made to order, so please book ahead and we will have it ready when you arrive.
